## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for critical clinical pharmacotherapy decision support within Costa Rica's healthcare ecosystem. The brand personality is forensic, authoritative, quiet, and deeply human. It rejects consumer wellness cheerfulness, hyperactive tech tropes, and the pervasive "clinical SaaS blue" stereotype in favor of a grounded, archival intelligence environment.

The design movement combines **Minimalism** with an **Architectural / Editorial Workspace**:
- **Graphite framing**: Deep charcoal chrome frames the workspace to establish a quiet operational perimeter, reducing visual glare during extended clinical shifts.
- **Bone surfaces**: Warm, natural cream paper tones replace sterile laboratory white for primary operational surfaces, easing ocular fatigue and improving long-form readability.
- **Aubergine accents**: Reserved for institutional brand presence, non-critical primary actions, and synthetic intelligence synthesis.
- **Strict semantic quarantine**: Red, amber, and forest green are strictly isolated to diagnostic urgency, medical risk, and data presence. They are never repurposed for decorative or marketing elements.

Core operational tenets:
1. *El sistema apoya la decisión; el profesional toma la decisión.* (The system supports; the clinician decides.)
2. *Dato no disponible ≠ normal.* Missing clinical data is an explicit clinical risk, never an assumed negative or safe default.
3. Every automated insight, rule generation, and AI interpretation maintains immediate evidence traceability back to institutional guidelines, GRADE ratings, and clinical audit logs.

## Colors

The color palette establishes an uncompromising separation between structural navigation, brand identity, and diagnostic data.

### Structural & Surface Tones
- **Graphite (950–700)**: Anchors dark navigation bars, global sidebars, and high-level frame architecture.
- **Bone (50–200, White)**: Provides warmth and clarity across clinical panels, patient headers, medication registries, and laboratory tables. Pure white is reserved for high-focus reading containers and interactive input fields.

### Interaction & Brand Identity
- **Aubergine (700–100)**: Serves as the singular non-clinical hue. It denotes user-driven actions, system focus rings, active navigation items, and AI-assisted clinical synthesis. Saturated blues are prohibited anywhere in the interface.

### Semantic Clinical Architecture
Clinical alerts always adhere to a tripartite chromatic model comprising text/icon foreground, surface fill, and structural border:
- **Critical / High (`#C83F49`)**: High-risk drug interactions, contraindications, and severe patient harm alerts.
- **Warning / Medium (`#C88728`)**: Dose adjustments, moderate drug-disease interactions, and special monitoring cautions.
- **Safe / Confirmed (`#3F765B`)**: Reconciled regimens, normal organ function metrics, and safe combinations.
- **Low / Informational (`#6D6672`)**: Subtle guidance, standard monitoring intervals, and operational history.
- **Missing / Unknown (`#B97821`)**: Explicit uncertainty banner (*Dato no disponible ≠ normal*). Never rendered as neutral or normal.

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy splits between authoritative humanistic editorial accents and high-density, error-intolerant clinical data representation.

- **Primary UI & Data Typography (`Inter`)**: All operational data, dosage calculations, patient identifiers, alert feeds, laboratory measurements, and rule audit logs utilize `Inter`. OpenType tabular numerals (`tnum`) and slashed zeros are strictly enabled across all numeric output to prevent decimal misalignment in complex pharmacotherapy tables.
- **Editorial / Institutional Framing (`Source Serif 4`)**: Applied sparingly to product titles, institutional slogans, and primary workspace headers. It is explicitly prohibited inside data tables, prescription badges, and clinical rationale panels to preserve scan speed.
- **Character Line Length**: Explanatory clinical paragraphs and AI rationales must be constrained between 65 and 85 characters per line to minimize saccadic fatigue during diagnostic reading.
- **Capitalization**: All-caps styling is exclusively permitted on short status tags (`ALTA`, `MEDIA`, `BAJA`, `NO DISPONIBLE`). Never apply all-caps to medication names, alert headlines, or patient narratives.

## Layout & Spacing

The workspace implements a **Desktop-First (1440 × 900)** 3-tier clinical cockpit layout with a 4px base modular scale:

### Structure Architecture
1. **Persistent Global Navigation (220px fixed)**: Left-aligned Graphite dark bar hosting primary modules (`Dashboard`, `Pacientes`, `Revisión farmacoterapéutica`, `Alertas`, `Base de conocimiento`, `Auditoría`).
2. **Central Clinical Canvas (fluid, min 720px)**: Displays patient header summary, medication tables, critical alert triage lists, and rule provenance cards.
3. **Contextual Intelligence Panel (380px – 420px fixed)**: Right-aligned detail inspector hosting expandable alert dossiers, pharmacokinetic interaction mechanisms, and AI clinical summaries.

### Breakpoint Adaptations
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px)**: Full tri-pane workspace active with persistent lateral inspector.
- **Tablet Landscape (1024px – 1279px)**: Lateral inspector collapses into an overlay slide-out sheet; canvas gutters compress to `1rem`. Tables remain un-truncated.
- **Compact Viewport (< 1024px)**: Global sidebar collapses to an icon bar or drawer. Medication tables gain dedicated horizontal pan capabilities while keeping active medication names pinned.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids noisy atmospheric shadows, skeuomorphic gloss, and translucent glassmorphism. It uses an architectural surface-layering system relying primarily on structural borders and targeted ambient drop shadows:

- **Base Workspace Canvas**: `Bone 100` (`#F4EFE7`) serves as the primary ground layer.
- **Resting Panels & Cards**: Rendered in `Bone-White` (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px solid #E9E1D7` outline and subtle ambient depth: `box-shadow: 0 4px 14px rgba(24, 20, 28, 0.06)`.
- **Floating Modals & Dropdowns**: Elevated with `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px rgba(24, 20, 28, 0.12)` enclosed in a crisp `#DED7D0` stroke.
- **Interactive Focus & Active Stacking**: No glowing neons or pulsating borders. Focus states employ a crisp inner `#775382` aubergine line flanked by an outer `rgba(119, 83, 130, 0.18)` halo.

## Shapes

The interface balances precision and utility with disciplined, humanistic contours:

- **Default Border Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px)**: Standard inputs, actionable buttons, table wrappers, and interactive tiles.
- **Compact Elements (`0.25rem` – `0.375rem` / 4px – 6px)**: Inline action triggers, micro-badges, and code/rule ID containers.
- **Large Structural Containers (`0.75rem` / 12px)**: Main patient header banners, workspace panels, and AI audit summary cards.
- **Status Pills & Severe Badges (`999px`)**: Fully rounded capsules used exclusively for triage tags (`ALTA`, `MEDIA`), clinical condition labels, and patient allergy chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Solid Aubergine 600 (`#62406F`) background, white label, `8px` radius. Hover shifts to `#4D2F5C`. Never rendered in red or green.
- **Secondary**: Bone-White fill, `1px solid #B89BC4` border, Aubergine 700 text (`#4D2F5C`).
- **Ghost / Tertiary**: Transparent fill, Text Secondary (`#5F5962`), subtle hover in Bone 200 (`#E9E1D7`).
- **Clinical Danger Action**: Reserved strictly for therapeutic discontinuations or severe alert overrides; `#C83F49` fill with explicit confirmation modal.

### Input Fields & Search
- **Default Height**: `38px` to `42px`.
- **Structure**: Bone-White fill, `1px solid #D8D1CB` border, `8px` radius, `#242126` text, `#8B848C` placeholder.
- **Focus**: `1px solid #775382` with an ambient `rgba(119, 83, 130, 0.18)` ring. No blue browser-native focus outlines.

### Clinical Alert Banners & Cards
- **Structure**: Light surface tint, matching semantic border stroke, bold title, body explanation, rule citation.
- **Missing Data Banner**: Surface `#F6E8CA`, border `#DDB66D`, text `#B97821`. Must contain the mandatory phrase: **"Dato no disponible ≠ normal"**.

### Status Chips & Pills
- **Geometry**: Height `22px`–`24px`, horizontal padding `8px`, fully rounded (`999px`).
- **Severity Chips**:
  - `ALTA`: `#F8E5E7` fill, `#E9A8AE` border, `#C83F49` text.
  - `MEDIA`: `#F7EDD7` fill, `#E6C58D` border, `#C88728` text.
  - `BAJA`: `#EFECF0` fill, `#CFC8D2` border, `#6D6672` text.

### Medication & Data Tables
- **Metrics**: Row height `44px`–`48px`, header height `36px`.
- **Headers**: Muted graphite text `#7D767F`, 11px uppercase label styling, border bottom `1px solid #E9E1D7`.
- **Numerals**: Strict tabular digits for dose, frequency, and start dates.

### AI Explanation & Provenance Box
- **Identity**: Surface Aubergine 100 (`#EEE5F1`), border Aubergine 300 (`#B89BC4`).
- **Header**: Tagged with pill badge **"Generado con IA"** in Aubergine 600.
- **Footer Disclaimer**: Always appends standard ethical guidance: *"La IA complementa, no reemplaza, el juicio clínico profesional."*
